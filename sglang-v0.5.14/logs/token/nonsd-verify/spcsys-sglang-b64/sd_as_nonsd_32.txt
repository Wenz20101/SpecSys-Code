Sure! The surface area $ A $ of a sphere is given by the formula:

$$
A = 4\pi r^2
$$

Where:
- $ r $ is the radius of the sphere
- $ \pi $ is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14159

Here's a Python function to calculate the surface area of a sphere:

```python
import math

def sphere_surface_area(radius):
    """
    Calculate the surface area of a sphere.

    Parameters:
    radius (float): The radius of the sphere.

    Returns:
    float: The surface area of the sphere.
    """
    if radius < 0:
        raise ValueError("Radius cannot be negative.")
    return 4 * math.pi * (radius ** 2)
```

### Example usage:

```python
area = sphere_surface_area(5)
print(f"The surface area of the sphere is: {area:.2f}")
```

```
The surface area of the sphere is: 314.16
```
