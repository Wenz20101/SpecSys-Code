Sure! A **triangular prism** is a 3D shape with two congruent triangular bases connected by three rectangular sides. The **volume** of a triangular prism can be calculated using the formula:

$$
\text{Volume} = \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}
$$

Where:
- **Base Area** is the area of the triangular base, calculated as:
  $$
  \text{Base Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}
  $$
- **Height** is the distance between the two triangular bases (also called the length of the prism).

Here's a Python function to compute the volume of a triangular prism:

```python
def volume_of_triangular_prism(base, height_of_triangle, length_of_prism):
    """
    Calculate the volume of a triangular prism.

    Parameters:
    base (float): The base length of the triangular face.
    height_of_triangle (float): The height of the triangular face.
    length_of_prism (float): The length of the prism (distance between the two triangular bases).

    Returns:
    float: The volume of the triangular prism.
    """
    base_area = 0.5 * base * height_of_triangle
    volume = base_area * length_of_prism
    return volume
```

### Example usage:

```python
# Example: base = 5, height_of_triangle = 3, length_of_prism = 10
vol = volume_of_triangular_prism(5, 3, 10)
print("Volume of the triangular prism:", vol)
```
